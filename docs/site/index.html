Objective

Given multiple distributors, their freight obligations, available vehicles, a shared transportation network, and contractual constraints — produce an admissible shared-load allocation and an auditable financial settlement.

Killer loop

3 shipments ↓ 1 shared truck ↓ MultiFlow validates ↓ cost allocation (deterministic) ↓ ledger ↓ auditable settlement

Principles

Never fork MultiFlow

FreightFlow supplies the domain model and translates it into a MultiFlow problem. MultiFlow validates; FreightFlow interprets commercially.

Deterministic money

No LLM or optimizer invents financial numbers. Explicit allocation policies (weight, volume, composite) produce exact shares recorded on the settlement.

Explainable ledger

Every charge answers: Why does participant X owe $Y? — contract + shipment + vehicle + policy + validated allocation.

Conflict is a feature

Exclusive carrier, over-capacity, non-shareable freight — rejected with clear constraint codes. Generate → detect → explain.
